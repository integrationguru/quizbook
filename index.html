River: Colorado Landform: Canyon

Its water once reached the Gulf of California but now rarely does, drained by seven states under a 1922 compact. It carved a 1.8-kilometre-deep gorge across the Colorado Plateau over roughly six million years. For the points, name this river whose canyon is a United States national park.
